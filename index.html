Photo & paper

Photograph your object lying flat on a sheet of paper, all four paper corners visible. The paper's known size corrects perspective/skew and sets the real-world scale.

Drag the four handles onto the paper corners — the yellow edge is the paper's top. Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan, double-click to fit.
