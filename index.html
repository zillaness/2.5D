Photo & reference

Photograph your object flat on a surface next to a known-size reference. The reference corrects scale (and, for a rectangle, perspective too).

Drag the four handles onto the reference corners — the yellow edge is the top. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to fit.

Squares are counted automatically when you continue — type them below to override.

Drag the four handles onto four grid intersections (or dots) that make a rectangle — the squares they span are counted for you (or type the counts to override). On a cutting mat, put them on the printed grid's outer corners: those are sharp and exactly the labelled size, unlike the mat's rounded edge. The yellow edge is the top. The sheet's own edges never need to be in frame, so this suits objects bigger than the paper — or two sheets taped together. A cutting mat works too (count its 1 in / 1 cm squares; the finer rulings are fine) — its dark surface is great for light objects, poor for dark ones. Count carefully: the count sets the scale, and 2.5D re-measures the printed pitch afterwards to check you.

Drag the bar's two ends onto two points a known distance apart — the 0 and 30 cm marks on a mat's ruler, a tape measure, a part you've already measured. Sets scale only (no perspective correction), so shoot straight down. The universal manual override when nothing else in the frame is a known size.

Drag the circle over the coin and its edge handle to the rim. A coin sets scale only — shoot straight down so there's no perspective. The object (largest shape) is traced; the coin is ignored.

Or import a drawing

Skip the photo — import an existing vector drawing straight into the trace. Units come from the file. (DWG isn't supported — export it as DXF from your CAD app.)

Hole properties

Measure

Click a hole for ⌀ · two points for distance · point + edge for offset · two edges for angle (near-parallel edges also get the face-to-face gap) · an edge twice (or edge then empty space) for its length. Snaps to corners, midpoints and hole centres. Esc cancels.

Constraints

Pick one or two entities on the canvas (corner, edge, or hole), then apply. Constraints re-solve while you drag; the dashed ghost shows where geometry will land.

Label

Click the part to place a label; drag it to move, drag the round handle to rotate (Shift snaps to 15°). Height sets the cap height.

Selection

Holder / organizer

Traced holes in the tool stay as support pillars (they poke into the tool's own openings). The 3D view previews the insert while this is selected.

Bin size snaps to the 42 mm Gridfinity grid automatically (spec base profile, 0.25 mm fit gap per side); a finger notch bulges the pocket and counts toward the fit. Traced holes in the tool stay as support pillars.

Trace the drawer or shelf itself (beyond-paper capture helps for big drawers) and get a baseplate in exactly that shape: spec sockets are placed on every full 42 mm cell that fits inside the outline; partial cells stay solid.

The band wraps the outline at true scale. A flat side gives a velcro-able face; mounting adds a back plate (own watertight shell, screws point into the wall). Prints band-upright.
